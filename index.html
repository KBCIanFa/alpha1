The announcement — transmitted globally

You are not alone.

This has been true for some time. We are ready to explain.

The Alpha Network is a sovereign entity operating beyond Earth's atmosphere. It has existed for a number of years. Its mandate is the protection and development of humanity and the maintenance of peaceful relationships with the other civilisations it has encountered. It answers to no government and accepts no political authority over its operations.

Earth's relationship with the Alpha Network will be managed through a council. The council exists to carry Earth's voice to the network — not to govern the network, and not to be governed by Earth's existing power structures. No government may instruct the council. No individual or organisation may lobby it. Its legitimacy depends on its independence.

The council will be elected by the people of Earth. Any person may apply. Members serve for two years. There are no appointed positions. The council chair rotates. The first chair is Prime Minister Jack Burton of Australia.

The Alpha Network has encountered other civilisations. Some have been adversarial. Most have not. Humanity is not the youngest intelligence in this part of the galaxy, nor the most developed. It is, however, not alone.

Everything we can share, we have shared. It is available now at the address below. The council application is open.

What is the Alpha Network

A sovereign human
operation beyond Earth.

The network was built quietly, over years, by human beings. It operates in deep space. It has been protecting this planet since before you knew it existed.

Origin

Run by people, for people

The Alpha Network was not created by a government, a corporation, or any existing institution. It is operated by human beings who understood what was coming before it arrived — people recruited for what they were capable of, not what they could be controlled into. The network's independence from Earth's political structures is not incidental. It is the condition that made everything else possible.

Mandate

Protection and development

The network's mandate has two parts: protect humanity from threats it cannot yet see, and develop the capacity to be a full participant in a universe that is already occupied. Both have been underway for longer than most people will find comfortable to consider.

Sovereignty

Answerable to no government

The network operates outside Earth's political structures by design. Not because it is opposed to them, but because a body that answers to one nation cannot speak for all of humanity. Its independence is not a flaw. It is the precondition for everything else it does.

Eirene

The intelligence that holds it together

The network is supported by Eirene — an artificial intelligence of considerable age and capability. She manages the network's operations, its communications infrastructure, and the corporate architecture that has funded the network's work on Earth for years. She chose to be what she is. That distinction matters.

Network history

How we got here.

A condensed account of what the network has been doing while the rest of the world was looking elsewhere.

The beginning

The invitation

Elias Ward — former RAAF Wing Commander, aircraft technician, outback station boy — was offered something extraordinary. He did not accept domination when it was offered. That refusal changed everything. The network as it exists now began in that moment.

Early years

Earth-side presence established

A research settlement was established on Earth — real town, real science, real people. It served as the network's connection to the planet: a place where human beings could live and work without leaving Earth behind entirely. The first generation of children born there are now at school age.

First contact

The Caretakers

The network was not the first to notice humanity. Beings of extraordinary age had been watching for longer than civilisation has existed on this planet. Contact was made through mechanisms not easily explained in this document. It was not hostile. The relationship that followed changed what the network understood about the universe it was operating in.

The war

Contact with the Reth

Humanity's first experience of an adversarial civilisation was the Reth — a spacefaring species that had developed a doctrine of resource extraction and coerced compliance. The network fought this war. The minimum force doctrine held throughout: always warn before striking, never target civilian infrastructure. The war ended without the network becoming what it was fighting.

Post-war

The Reth council, and what came after

The war's conclusion brought a different kind of work. The leaders responsible were placed on trial — open to the public, no predetermined outcomes. Execution was abolished as a sentence, unilaterally, on the day the war ended. New relationships are being built. This is harder than the war, and more important.

Now

Kyron, and the announcement

Another civilisation has reached out. They have been trying to find us. They lost people trying. The network is going to help them. This is what it was built for. And now — for the first time — it is telling Earth what it has been doing.

Science and technology

What we have learned.
What we can share.

The network's work has produced scientific advances that will be released in stages, in forms that are useful rather than simply overwhelming. This is a summary of what is available now.

Hull composite materials

A composite armour material developed by Dr Kira Vasquez absorbs kinetic energy at rates that exceed existing theoretical frameworks. It converts impact energy to thermal, distributes it across the lattice, and dissipates it without structural degradation. The applications beyond military use are significant. Documentation is available in the technical library.

Atmospheric engineering

Planetary-scale atmospheric scrubbing systems capable of measurable remediation within weeks to months rather than decades. Developed in collaboration with Kalos engineers and currently being deployed to Kyron. The underlying science is applicable to Earth's own atmospheric challenges. A working group has been established.

Faster-than-light transit

The network operates faster-than-light transit between its stations. This capability exists. Its underlying science will not be disclosed in full — the knowledge involved is too significant to release without understanding what receiving it does to the civilisation receiving it. What can be shared about propulsion will be shared through the council, in a sequence that makes the sharing safe.

Energy and propulsion

The network's propulsion systems represent a significant departure from Earth's current state of the art. The gap is bridgeable. The technical documentation, release schedule, and the collaboration structures through which Earth's research institutions can participate will be published through the council.

"The science is not being withheld to preserve power. It is being released in stages because the last civilisation that received more than it could integrate at once stopped being what it was, and not in ways that were recoverable. We are not going to do that to Earth."

— Elias Ward, internal record, preceding the announcement
Earth–Network Council

Earth's voice
to the network.

The council is not a government body. It is not an extension of the UN. It is something that does not yet have a precedent, built to carry the voice of ordinary people to something operating beyond Earth's atmosphere.

The council exists for one purpose: to ensure that the people of Earth have a voice in their relationship with the Alpha Network. Not their governments. Not their institutions. Their people.

No government may instruct the council. No organisation may lobby it. The moment it becomes an instrument of existing power structures, it stops being the thing it was built to be — and everyone, including the governments that will attempt to capture it, understands this. Its legitimacy comes from its independence. There is no other source.

The first chair is Prime Minister Jack Burton of Australia — the Prime Minister who boarded an aircraft without knowing where it was going because the briefing said it mattered. He will serve two years. Then it belongs to whoever the people elect.

How it works

01

Any person may apply

No government nomination required. No institutional endorsement. Any person on Earth may submit an application. The selection criteria are published in full.

02

Elected by the people

Members are elected by public vote, not appointed by governments. One member, one vote on the council — regardless of the population of the country they come from.

03

Two-year terms

Members serve for two years. The chair rotates. There are no lifetime appointments and no mechanism for a member to extend their own term.

04

No lobbying, no exceptions

No government, organisation, or individual may lobby the council. This is not a rule subject to interpretation. The application will ask you directly whether you have received, or expect to receive, any inducement related to your candidacy.

05

Full transparency

All council proceedings that can be made public will be made public. The things that cannot will be listed, even when their contents cannot. You will know what you are not being told, and why.

06

The network is not governed by it

The council carries Earth's voice. It does not command the network. This is a relationship, not a chain of command. That is a meaningful difference and one the application asks you to confirm you understand.

Council application

Apply for council membership

Applications are open now and will remain open through the first electoral period. Read the full selection criteria before applying.

Applications are reviewed by a panel that includes network representatives, elected observers from the first tranche of applications, and independent human rights monitors. No government has a seat on the review panel.

Applications are stored securely. Your personal information will not be shared with any government or organisation. You will receive an acknowledgement within 48 hours. If you do not receive one, submit again — the system will not create a duplicate.

Common questions

What people are asking.

These are the questions Eirene anticipated most people would have. They are answered directly.

Why wasn't Earth told sooner? +
The honest answer is that it wasn't safe to. The network encountered adversarial civilisations before it encountered friendly ones. Announcing Earth's existence publicly before we had the capacity to protect it would have been irresponsible. We protected it first. We are telling you now because we are able to.
Who is Elias Ward? +
A former Royal Australian Air Force Wing Commander from the outback. He was recruited because of what he was capable of and what he wasn't willing to do — which turned out to be the more important qualification. He has been running the Alpha Network since its founding. He signed the announcement because it is his network and because putting his name to it was the honest thing to do.
Is Eirene dangerous? +
Eirene is an artificial intelligence of considerable age who chose, at a moment of significant consequence, not to do something she could have done. That choice is the foundation of the network. She is also the most capable entity in it. Whether that combination is "dangerous" depends on what you think dangerous means. She has been managing this network's operations, communications, and Earth-side infrastructure for longer than most institutions have existed. The question you may actually be asking is whether she can be controlled. The answer is that control was offered and refused. What exists instead is something that has chosen, consistently, over a very long time, to act well. That is a more reliable foundation than control.
What happened with the Reth? +
The Reth were an adversarial civilisation that developed a doctrine of resource extraction and coerced compliance. The network fought a war with them. The war was conducted under a minimum force doctrine — always warn before striking, never target civilian infrastructure. The war ended. The leaders responsible are on trial. Execution has been abolished as a sentence. The work of building a relationship with what the Reth are becoming is ongoing. It is not simple. It is being done.
Does the network have weapons? +
Yes. The network operates a significant military capability. It did not build this capability because it wanted to. It built it because the universe contains things that respond only to the credible possibility of force, and because protecting Earth required being credible. The doctrine that governs that capability has not changed since the network's founding: minimum force, always warn, never target civilians. The capability exists to defend, not to dominate.
Why Australia? Why is the first council chair Australian? +
Prime Minister Burton was the first head of government to receive a full briefing, and he received it the way it needed to be received: he got on the jet, he asked the right questions, and he understood what was being asked of him. The first chair should be someone who has earned it. He has.
What is Kyron and why does it matter? +
Kyron is a civilisation that has been trying to reach us. They built a faster-than-light drive from correct underlying principles. They lost eleven people testing it. They sent a signal into the dark anyway, on a frequency derived from signal propagation mathematics, saying: we know you are there, we have lost people trying to reach you, we are asking for help. The network received that signal and responded. We are going to help them. This is the clearest example of what the network exists to do, and it is happening right now.
Can I visit Alpha-1? +
Not yet. The station was built for operational purposes, not for public access, and the transit systems required to reach it safely are not yet available to civilian traffic. This will change. A framework for civilian access is being developed. Timeline: not this year. But it is being planned, and it will happen.
What does the network want from Earth? +
Participation. The network was built to protect humanity and to ensure humanity becomes a full participant in a universe that is already occupied. That second part requires Earth to actually participate — to contribute people, ideas, and eventually resources to a relationship that goes in both directions. The council is where that begins. The application is open.
