Network fleet

The ships.

The fleet exists because the universe contains things that respond only to the credible possibility of force, and because protecting Earth required being credible. The doctrine has not changed since the network's founding: minimum force, always warn before striking, never target civilian infrastructure.

Command Station
Alpha-1
Flagship

A perfect sphere. No windows. No gun ports. No external features that are not load-bearing. Interior lighting comes from within the deck material itself — no overhead fixtures, no shadows. Gravity is Earth-standard throughout. A garden grows inside, built from soil carried from Earth. It was not built to be seen. It was built to last.

LocationJupiter orbit

Defensive Platform
Ward's Folly
Earth sector — active

An Earth-sector defensive platform, approximately half the scale of Alpha-1. Fully armoured. Adaptive shielding. Significant weapons capability. It was not requested or authorised. It was presented to the network as a fait accompli by its builder. Ward has described it as "a warning with engines." He kept it.

LocationEarth's solar system

Allied civilisation
Kalos
Active alliance

Green and gold. Deep blue water — not quite the blue of Earth's oceans. Richer, more deliberate. Everything old on Kalos is built from colonial stone, quarried from the cliffs and cut by hand by the original settlers. The network's relationship with Kalos is its closest. They sent engineering teams to Kyron without being asked.

GovernmentParliamentary democracy
Alliance statusFull — bilateral
First contact — active
Kyron
Assistance underway

Warm, wet, heavily populated. Two centuries of industrial activity have degraded the atmosphere to the point where sustained surface exposure is no longer safe without protection. The people of Kyron did this the way all civilisations have done it — solving the problem in front of them before they understood the cost. Atmospheric remediation is underway. The fires in the northern zone have gone out.

AtmosphereDegraded — remediation active
Remediation timelineWeeks to months
Kyron system — moon
Kyron Minor
Population relocated — refitting

A managed atmosphere. A crater-floor dome network and one hundred thousand people who held it together for sixteen years on the strength of a geothermal tap and a refusal to stop. The people who built it found it beautiful. They have moved to the residential stations. They intend to return to the surface of Kyron when it is ready for them.

Peak population100,000

Reth Home World
Post-war — trials ongoing

Blue-grey at orbital distance, banded cloud cover, the quality of a world that has been spacefaring for a long time. The council chambers where the decisions that shaped the war were made are now a construction site. The parklands being built there bear a name Dara Vel chose personally. The trials are ongoing, open to the public, with no predetermined outcomes. This is harder than the war. It is more important.

GovernmentCouncil of Eleven
TrialsOngoing — open proceedings

The specific quality of something built by an intelligence that has never needed to impress anyone. The network has been permitted to visit. This is not offered to everyone.
